Research

A unifying goal is a predictive description of quantum materials beyond idealized zero-temperature, perfectly ordered models. Current directions connect thermal ensembles, relativistic electronic structure, and chemical disorder within systematically improvable many-body approaches.

Beyond the ground state

Thermofield formulations of coupled cluster and related wave-function methods for finite-temperature electronic structure. The focus is on electron correlation, thermal ensembles, and electronic free energies, with applications to adsorption and phase stability.

Correlation, relativity & topology

Correlated topological phases in materials with strong spin–orbit coupling, described using fully self-consistent relativistic GW. Symmetry-resolved Green’s functions connect electronic spectra, orbital character, and band inversion in strain-tunable α-Sn.

Disorder in quantum materials

First-principles descriptions of disorder using coherent potential approximation with Bloch Gaussian orbitals. The focus is on the interplay of local chemical environments and electronic structure within a Green’s-function framework for materials beyond the perfect crystal.
