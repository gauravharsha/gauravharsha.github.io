{{< cv-section title="Teaching & Mentoring" id="teaching-mentoring" >}}
I have co-supervised student research in finite-temperature many-body theory and correlated topology:

- **Yi Xu**, undergraduate intern from Zhejiang University: thermal coupled cluster for spin systems, leading to a co-authored [Physical Review B paper](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.105.045125).
- **Selina Dirnböck**, master's intern from TU Wien: topological α-Sn using self-consistent GW, leading to a co-authored [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.22740).

Both students have gone on to doctoral study. I have also onboarded 10–20 users of the GREEN software suite across our group and collaborating institutions.

At Rice University, I served as a teaching assistant for **Introduction to Quantum Chemistry** in Fall 2018, 2019, and 2020, designing and grading homework and exams and delivering selected lectures. I also taught introductory physics laboratory sections for engineering and pre-medical students over four semesters.
